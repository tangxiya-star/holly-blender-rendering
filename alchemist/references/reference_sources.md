## Visual observations

Dome: dark, almost black lower walls and cabinetry; rounded squared-U counter segments on both sides, not concentric circular tables; centerline rectangular stone-topped service islands; blue-gray mottled stone; small slender arched warm task lamps; navy/black upholstered chairs; warm under-counter glow; far rear glazed shadow kitchen; broad continuous projection surface above a level horizontal rim. The Dinesen source states the dome is 18 m in diameter.

Balcony lounge: broad dark cocoa timber boards, plum upholstered wingback chair with tapered timber legs, black/charred timber panels and dark ceiling, mottled brass/gold bar front and backsplash, dense irregular hanging gold leaf lights. A visible gray masonry oven/chimney sits behind the bar. The photograph gives a bar-facing view rather than a complete lounge floor plan.
